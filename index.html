What happens after someone sees my Instagram ad?

They message your business on WhatsApp, choose a service and available time, confirm the details, and receive a payment option.

When is a booking confirmed?

Only after the connected backend verifies payment. A customer confirmation, Google Sheet update, and owner notification follow.

Can the owner cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes. The demonstrated journey includes owner approval, choosing a new slot, and requesting a refund under your cancellation policy.

Does this demo take real payments?

No. The video and interactive demo use fictional data. No real money, bookings, or WhatsApp messages are sent. Live operation requires provider configuration.
